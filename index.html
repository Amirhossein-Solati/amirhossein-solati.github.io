Research Interests

My research lies at the intersection of Wireless Communications and Artificial Intelligence, aiming to develop intelligent frameworks for Beyond 5G (B5G) and 6G networks. Specifically:

  • Intelligent Wireless Networks: Edge Intelligence, Network Slicing, and 6G Architecture.
  • UAV Communications: Trajectory Optimization and Resource Allocation using Mathematical Programming (SCA, Convex Optimization).
  • AI for Communications: Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) for dynamic network management.
  • Advanced Technologies: Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces (RIS) and ISAC.

Selected Academic Projects

UAV Selection and Trajectory OptimizationMATLAB, CVX

Modeled a non-convex optimization problem to jointly optimize UAV selection and 3D trajectory planning for maximizing energy efficiency in multi-UAV networks. Solved the formulation by implementing the Successive Convex Approximation (SCA) algorithm utilizing MATLAB optimization toolboxes.

End-to-End Learning for Physical Layer SystemsPython, Sionna, TensorFlow

Utilized the Sionna library's built-in autoencoder to model communication systems as end-to-end neural networks. Implemented the methodology from the recent ‘NoProb’ study to evaluate neural network-based signal detection and probabilistic shaping.

Performance Analysis of MIMO Coding SchemesPython

Simulated Space-Time Block Coding (STBC) architectures, evaluating Alamouti, orthogonal, and non-orthogonal designs. Analyzed Bit Error Rate (BER) performance over Rayleigh fading channels to assess diversity gains against conventional SISO systems.

Implementation of Applied Optimization AlgorithmsMATLAB

Programmed and analyzed foundational optimization algorithms from scratch, including gradient descent, as well as heuristic methods such as Genetic Algorithm (GA) and Ant Colony Optimization (ACO) for solving complex search space problems.

Design and Simulation of UWB Microstrip AntennaHFSS, CST Studio

Designed a compact ultra-wideband antenna with notch filtering capability for interference suppression; validated the radiation pattern and return loss through full-wave simulations.
